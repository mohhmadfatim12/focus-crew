# 01. Product Vision & Principles

## 1. App Purpose

**FocusCrew** (provisional name) is an offline-first Android application designed to help students, developers, and knowledge workers enter deep flow states by blocking distracting apps during focused study and work sessions.

Unlike aggressive, punitive focus apps, FocusCrew is built around **calm focus, transparency, and honesty**. When Android OS restrictions or system battery savers interrupt blocking, FocusCrew does not pretend everything is fine—it tells the user clearly and honestly what happened and helps them resume smoothly.

---

## 2. Core Product Principles

### 1. Calm & Non-Judgmental
- The interface avoids harsh alarmist colors, loud buzzers, or shaming messages.
- The block screen is a gentle reminder that invites you back to your goal, rather than treating you like a prisoner.

### 2. Free Forever
- No paywalls, no trial periods, no subscription pop-ups, and no "pro version" locks on essential productivity features.
- All core timer modes, custom durations, and app blocking profiles are completely free to use.

### 3. Offline-First & No Account Needed
- Works 100% offline out of the box.
- Requires no sign-up, email, password, phone number, or cloud sync.
- Your study habits, blocked apps, and distraction entries stay entirely on your personal device.

### 4. No Addictive or Exploitative Design
- Zero gamification traps designed to drive artificial retention (no manipulative streaks, no guilt notifications, no loot-box rewards).
- The goal of FocusCrew is to help you put your phone down and do real work, not spend more time inside FocusCrew.

### 5. Honest Permissions
- We ask only for permissions strictly required to perform the core task.
- We never read private message contents, screen text, or keystrokes.
- We explain in clear English exactly why a permission is requested before directing the user to Android Settings.

### 6. User Control & Emergency Exit
- The user is an adult in control of their own device.
- Strict mode introduces thoughtful friction (like a 30-second cooldown and confirmation prompt) to curb mindless impulses, but **never** traps the user.
- An emergency exit is always available. If taken, the session is honestly recorded as "interrupted" in your history.

---

## 3. Target Audience

- **Students & Learners:** Needing uninterrupted blocks for exam revision, reading, and problem-solving.
- **Deep Workers & Programmers:** Requiring extended, flow-state intervals without algorithmic social media interruptions.
- **Privacy-Conscious Individuals:** Users who refuse apps that track usage statistics, sell browsing habits, or require cloud logins.
