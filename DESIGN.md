# Design System: FocusCrew — Old Money Quiet Luxury (Daylight Editorial Edition)

## 1. Visual Atmosphere & Philosophy
This design system reimagines FocusCrew under **Warm Daylight Luxury**—the sensory feeling of morning sunlight entering an archival Mayfair library or a serene British country house.

- **Rejection of Harsh Contrast:** Pure black (`#000000`) and sterile white (`#FFFFFF`) are strictly banned as primary fields.
- **Warm Cashmere & Linen Base:** Canvas is a calm warm alabaster (`#FBF9F5`) and card containers are tactile linen creams (`#F2EEE5`, `#FFFFFF`).
- **Soft Editorial Typography:** Text is rendered in deep warm espresso ink (`#202621`) and soft muted charcoal-olive (`#5E665F`). Headlines use optical grade, soft-curved *Fraunces* serif; body text uses smooth, rounded *Plus Jakarta Sans*; numerals use monospace *JetBrains Mono*.
- **Quiet Luxury Accents:** Warm honey brass (`#A37A3E`) and archival British racing sage (`#284B39`). High friction states use gentle warm terracotta clay (`#8F3B3B`).
- **Tactile Elevation:** Soft, warm, diffused shadows (`0 8px 24px rgba(40, 48, 42, 0.05)`) with fine 1px warm sand/brass hairline borders.

## 2. Color Palette & Functional Roles
1. **Cashmere Cream Canvas** (`#FBF9F5`) — Soft daylight background, restful on eyes.
2. **Daylight Paper Surfaces** (`#FFFFFF` & `#F2EEE5`) — Elevated card containers with subtle pillowy depth.
3. **Soft Espresso Ink** (`#202621`) — Primary text and numbers. Never harsh black.
4. **Muted Charcoal-Olive** (`#5E665F`) — Secondary labels, subtitles, and telemetry hallmarks.
5. **Antique Honey Brass** (`#A37A3E`) — Active chronometers, primary buttons, knurled toggles, and highlights.
6. **British Racing Sage** (`#284B39`) — Category badges, peaceful focus indicators.
7. **Terracotta Clay** (`#8F3B3B`) — 30s friction gate, emergency cooldown, and warnings.
8. **Whisper Hairline Border** (`rgba(163, 122, 62, 0.16)`) — Precision 1px framing.

## 3. Typographic Hierarchy
- **Chronometer Countdown:** `JetBrains Mono` (56sp, Medium) in Soft Espresso.
- **Editorial Headlines:** `Fraunces` (30sp, Medium, Optically Balanced) with relaxed warmth.
- **Body & Actions:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (14sp–15sp, Regular/Medium) for approachable readability.
- **Telemetry Labels:** `JetBrains Mono` (11sp, All-Caps, +0.1em Letter-Spacing) in Honey Brass.

## 4. Component Archetypes
- **The Daylight Chronometer:** Fine circular watch dial with warm sand tick marks, soft espresso countdown numbers (`50:00`), and honey brass status pill.
- **The Distraction Vault:** Crisp white linen cards with 16px rounded corners, app icon cameos in warm sage frames, and tactile brass toggles.
- **The 30-Second Reflection Screen:** Calming warm terracotta-tinted parchment card with a thoughtful reflection prompt in soft Fraunces italics and a smooth unwinding breathing dial.
- **The Focus Ledger:** Cream financial ledger style with warm honey gold chronograph bar charts and olive wax-seal badges.
- **The Fortress Protocol:** Elegant safety settings card with honey brass segmented selectors and an unbreachable emergency essentials whitelist.
